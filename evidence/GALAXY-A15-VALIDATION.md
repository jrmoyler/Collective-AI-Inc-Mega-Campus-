# Galaxy A15 and desktop capture

Use PR 8's Vercel Preview link while signed into the permitted Vercel account. Do not test the unchanged main deployment by mistake.

1. Record phone model, Android/Chrome versions, battery/power-saving state, orientation and time. Allow the phone to cool first. Test the default Balanced mode before High detail.
2. Open Systems → Record device performance. Close Systems and explore for at least 60 seconds: aerial orbit, Prism exterior, Trust Vault exterior, mesh/energy/fleet toggles. Capture screenshots at the same angles as the source references.
3. Enter Trust Vault. Select Visitor demonstration, Identity operations and Credential lab; walk and drag-look; change to L02; exit and re-enter. Repeat with Prism, Eden Spire, Titan Works and Care Village. Check that all UI controls remain usable in portrait and landscape.
4. Return to campus → Systems → Finish & download report. The JSON is generated on the device; it is not sent anywhere. Attach it with screenshots/screen recording and the model/conditions from step 1.
5. Repeat after 10 minutes of continuous use to expose thermal degradation. Then repeat on desktop at a recorded viewport and rendering-quality setting.

The report contains frame intervals grouped by engine/facility/floor/quality, mean FPS, p95 frame time and render-buffer dimensions. It excludes pauses of at least two seconds and caps the collection at 18,000 samples. It does not measure GPU time or memory, and user-agent information alone does not prove physical hardware.

Proposed performance acceptance: sustained mean ≥30 FPS and p95 frame time ≤50 ms in Balanced mode, no context loss, crashes, stuck navigation or unreadable controls. Report slow segments rather than averaging them away. Visual acceptance is separate: reference placement, individual silhouette, facade/material response, furnished visible spaces, landscape and connected systems must each be checked. Unseen-room furnishing is an interpretation under the user's approved direction.
